ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Hostel operations, made simpler.

HOSTEL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM is a college-project web application that brings common hostel workflows into one secure, easy-to-use portal.

Our objective

Help hostel administrators maintain accurate student records, manage rooms and allocations, track fee records, respond to complaints and publish notices without relying on disconnected spreadsheets.

Built for two roles
  • Students: manage profile details, see their allocated room and fees, submit complaints and read notices.
  • Admin/Warden: manage students, rooms, allocations, fee records, complaints and notices.
